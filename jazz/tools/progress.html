Suggested milestones

Milestone What it means
Modules 1–3 complete You read tab and notation, know the fretboard to fret 12, and can build any triad and seventh chord.
Modules 4–6 complete You can comp a jazz blues with shell voicings in swing time and improvise with chord tones.
Modules 7–9 complete You play drop-2 and drop-3 voicings through ii–V–I in all keys and improvise with the major, melodic-minor and symmetric scales.
Modules 10–12 complete You have bebop vocabulary, understand reharmonisation, and know 20+ standards from memory.
Modules 13–15 complete Chord melody, modern harmony, and the professional skills to lead or join a band.
