Practice plans

Daily routines for 30, 60 and 120 minutes, and how they change as you move through the course.

Practice is where the course happens. Three principles underlie every plan below.

  • Rotate, don't pile up. Each session has fixed slots (technique, harmony, improvisation, repertoire, ear). Fill each slot with the current lesson's assignment.
  • Measure. Write the tempo you achieved. Tomorrow's job is +2 bpm or one more key.
  • Keep a log. Date, slots, tempos, what was hard. A log makes a month of work visible and keeps you honest.

The 30-minute plan (minimum effective dose)

Minutes Slot Examples
5 Warm-up & fretboard fretboard trainer two minutes, chromatic exercise with the metronome
10 Current lesson the lesson's exercise at the assigned tempo
5 Harmony voicings through ii–V–I in two new keys
5 Improvising / vocabulary one phrase in three keys over a backing track or the metronome on 2 & 4
5 Repertoire one tune from memory, melody and chords

Ear training (5 min) goes on the commute, in the ear trainer.

The 60-minute plan (serious hobbyist / student)

Minutes Slot
10 Technique: chromatic and scale exercises, picking patterns, with metronome (log tempo)
10 Fretboard & reading: sight-read 8–16 bars of new material (any book of melodies)
15 Harmony: current voicing lesson in all 12 keys, one string set
15 Improvisation: current concept over one tune; record 2 choruses, listen back
10 Repertoire: one new tune by ear, one old tune reviewed

Plus 10 minutes of ear training and 20+ minutes of focused listening every day.

The 120-minute plan (conservatory / professional preparation)

Minutes Slot
15 Technique and time (alternate picking, legato, sweep, chord-tone arpeggios across the neck, metronome on 2 & 4 at slow and fast tempos)
15 Sight-reading (single lines and chord charts; Module 15 lesson on reading)
20 Harmony: voicings, voice-leading, chord melody arranging
20 Vocabulary: transcription and transposing phrases through 12 keys
20 Improvisation over tunes (play-along or looper), recorded and reviewed
20 Repertoire: learning and maintaining a list of 50+ tunes
10 Ear training and singing

How the slots change through the course

Modules Technique Harmony Improvisation Repertoire
1–3 chromatic/major scales, picking, note names triads and seventh chords in root position none yet: sing scales and arpeggios learn 3 melodies by ear (simple songs)
4–7 arpeggios in position, swing eighths shell voicings, Freddie Green comping chord tones and guide tones over blues and ii–V–I Stage 1 list, 5 tunes
8–9 3-notes-per-string scales, position shifts drop-2 / drop-3 through tunes, inversions chord-scale practice, modes of melodic minor Stage 1 list complete (10 tunes)
10–12 bebop lines at tempo, articulation reharmonisation, substitutions on tunes bebop vocabulary, transcription Stage 2 list, 25 tunes
13–15 chord melody technique, hybrid picking chord melody arrangements, modern voicings motivic development, odd meters, free playing 40–60 tunes, including Stage 3
