How the shapes were made


Every diagram comes from the course's theory engine, not from a hand-typed chart. For each chord symbol the engine spells the chord, builds the close-position inversions, applies the drop-2 or drop-3 transformation, and then solves for the lowest playable fret positions on each string set (a hand span of five frets or less). An automated test plays every one of the 8,000-plus shapes back through the fretboard model and checks that the sounding notes are exactly the chord's notes. The same data feeds the voicing lessons in Modules 4 and 8.
